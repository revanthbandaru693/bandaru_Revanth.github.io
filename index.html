About & experience

Where I've trained, and the kind of work I'm chasing next.

I got into games the way a lot of environment artists do — by taking apart the worlds I liked playing in to understand why they felt believable. That curiosity turned into a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science and Game Development at ICAT Design and Media College, where I split my time between greyboxing levels, painting terrain, and wiring up UI that actually survives contact with a player.

I’m an XR Developer at Edgeforce Solutions and a Computer Science & Game Development student at ICAT Design & Media College. My work combines technology, game development, and immersive 3D experiences..

Through my work and studies, I’m continuously developing my skills in XR, game development, UI, 3D environments, and interactive experiences, with a goal of creating engaging digital worlds that connect technology with visual storytelling.
